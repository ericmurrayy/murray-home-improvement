Questions

About these numbers

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Massachusetts?+

As a general range, a mid-range kitchen remodel in the Chelmsford area often runs roughly $30,000–$75,000, with smaller refreshes below that and high-end or wall-down projects above it. Your itemized estimate is the only accurate number.

What drives kitchen remodel cost the most?+

Cabinetry and countertops usually represent the largest share, followed by layout changes (moving walls, plumbing, or electrical), appliances, and the level of finish you choose.

Is the estimate really free?+

Yes. We provide a fair, itemized estimate with a clear breakdown of costs and materials at no cost and no obligation.
