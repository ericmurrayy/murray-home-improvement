What a kitchen remodel costs

Kitchens are the most popular remodel for a reason — and also one of the most variable in price. The honest answer to “what will it cost” is: it depends on size, layout changes, and the finishes you choose. Below are general ranges to help you plan; the only accurate number is a free, itemized estimate.

Typical ranges in the Merrimack Valley

Refresh (paint, hardware, counters)$12k – $25k
Mid-range full remodel$30k – $55k
High-end / layout change$60k – $100k+
Cabinetry (share of budget)35–50%
Countertops & tile10–20%

Ranges are illustrative for planning only and vary by scope, materials, and site conditions.

What moves the price

Layout changes. Moving walls, relocating plumbing, or adding an island raises labor and trade costs. Cabinetry. Stock vs. semi-custom vs. fully custom is the single biggest lever. Counters & tile. Quartz and natural stone cost more than laminate; intricate tile work adds labor. Appliances & lighting. These can be modest or a major line item depending on your choices.

Where to save without regret

Keep the existing footprint when you can, invest in the things you touch every day (hardware, faucet, counters), and choose timeless finishes over trends. We’ll always show you where your dollars have the most impact.

Questions

Frequently asked

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Massachusetts?+

As a general range, a mid-range kitchen remodel in the Merrimack Valley often runs roughly $30,000–$75,000, with smaller refreshes below that and high-end or wall-down projects above it. Your itemized estimate is the only accurate number.

What drives kitchen remodel cost the most?+

Cabinetry and countertops usually represent the largest share, followed by layout changes (moving walls, plumbing, or electrical), appliances, and the level of finish you choose.

Can I remodel a kitchen on a smaller budget?+

Yes. Keeping the existing layout, refacing or re-using cabinets, and choosing durable mid-range finishes can meaningfully lower cost while still transforming the space.
