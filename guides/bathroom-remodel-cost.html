What a bathroom remodel costs

Bathrooms are small rooms with big requirements — plumbing, waterproofing, tile, ventilation, and electrical all in a tight footprint. That’s why cost-per-square-foot runs high. Here are general ranges to plan around, with the real number coming from a free estimate.

Typical ranges in the Merrimack Valley

Powder room / refresh$8k – $18k
Mid-range full bath$18k – $32k
Primary suite / curbless$35k – $60k+
Tile & waterproofing15–25%
Vanity, fixtures & glass15–25%

Ranges are illustrative for planning only and vary by scope, materials, and site conditions.

What moves the price

Tile & shower. Custom tile showers, curbless entries, and niches add labor. Layout. Moving the toilet, tub, or vanity means new plumbing runs. Heated floors. A popular New England upgrade that adds material and electrical. Fixtures & glass. Frameless glass and quality fixtures raise the finish level.

Smart places to invest

Waterproofing and ventilation are not where to cut — they’re what keeps the room sound for decades. Spend there, then choose finishes that match your taste and budget.

Questions

Frequently asked

How much does a bathroom remodel cost?+

A mid-range bathroom remodel in our area commonly runs roughly $15,000–$35,000, with powder-room refreshes below and large primary-suite or curbless-shower builds above. Your estimate is the accurate figure.

Why are bathrooms expensive per square foot?+

Bathrooms pack plumbing, waterproofing, tile, electrical, and ventilation into a small space — a lot of skilled trades per square foot, all of which must be done right to last.

Is a walk-in or curbless shower more expensive?+

Usually a bit more, because of the slope, drainage, and waterproofing involved — but it’s popular for both style and aging-in-place, and we build it to last.
