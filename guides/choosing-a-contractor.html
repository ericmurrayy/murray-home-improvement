How to choose a contractor

The contractor you choose matters more than any single material. A good one protects your home, your budget, and your peace of mind. Here’s what to look for — and what should give you pause.

Questions to ask

Are you licensed and insured? In Massachusetts, ask for the CSL and HIC numbers and proof of insurance. Do you provide itemized estimates? A clear breakdown of labor and materials signals transparency. Who’s on site? Know whether the owner is involved or it’s subcontracted out. Can I see recent work and references? Real projects and real clients tell the story. Who pulls the permits? A professional handles permitting and inspections.

Red flags to avoid

Pressure to sign immediately, large cash deposits up front, no written contract, no license or insurance, and bids dramatically lower than everyone else. If it feels off, it usually is.

Questions

Frequently asked

How do I verify a contractor in Massachusetts?+

Ask for their Construction Supervisor License (CSL) and Home Improvement Contractor (HIC) registration numbers, and confirm they carry insurance. You can look up licenses through the state.

Is the lowest bid the best choice?+

Rarely. The lowest bid often means lower-grade materials, missing scope, or corners cut. Compare itemized estimates line by line, not just bottom-line numbers.
