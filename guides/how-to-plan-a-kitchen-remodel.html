How to plan a kitchen remodel

A great kitchen remodel is won or lost in the planning. The build goes smoothly when the decisions are made up front. Here’s the path we walk every client through.

01

Set your priorities & budget

List what’s not working and what you must have. Decide a realistic budget range before falling in love with finishes.

02

Plan the layout

Work the “working triangle” — sink, stove, fridge. Decide early if walls move, since that drives cost and permits.

03

Choose cabinetry

The biggest line item and the longest lead time. Lock this first: stock, semi-custom, or custom.

04

Select counters, tile & fixtures

Coordinate finishes as a package so everything reads intentional. Order before demo.

05

Confirm appliances & lighting

Sizes affect cabinetry and electrical. Finalize before framing.

06

Schedule the build

With materials confirmed, we set a realistic timeline and keep your home livable throughout.

The most common mistake

Starting demo before decisions and materials are locked. It leads to a torn-up kitchen waiting on back-ordered parts. We plan first, build second — so the on-site phase is fast and predictable.

Questions

Frequently asked

How long does a kitchen remodel take?+

Most full kitchens run 4–8 weeks on site once materials are in hand. Planning and ordering beforehand often takes longer than the build itself.

Should I order materials before demo?+

Yes. We wait until cabinets, counters, and appliances are confirmed (and usually on site) before demo, so your kitchen isn’t torn up waiting on a back-ordered item.
