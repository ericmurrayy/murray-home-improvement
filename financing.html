How it works

Mention financing when you reach out. We’ll prepare your itemized estimate, then help you line it up with the funding option that fits — including coordinating documentation with your lender when needed.

Murray Home Improvement is not a lender. Financing is provided by third parties subject to their terms and approval.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do you offer financing?+

We can point you toward trusted home-improvement financing partners and work with your timeline. Ask us during your free estimate and we’ll walk through the options.

Will financing change my estimate?+

No. Your itemized estimate is the same whether you pay directly or finance — we believe in transparent, consistent pricing.

What about home equity options?+

Many homeowners fund larger remodels and additions through a HELOC or home-equity loan. We’re happy to coordinate with your lender on documentation and timelines.
