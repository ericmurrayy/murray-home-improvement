Do you guarantee your work?+

Yes. We stand behind our craftsmanship and address any workmanship issues that arise after a project. As an owner-operated company, our reputation rides on every job.

What about manufacturer warranties?+

Products like windows, roofing, and fixtures carry their own manufacturer warranties. We install to manufacturer specifications so those warranties stay valid, and we register them where required.

Fully. Murray Home Improvement is a licensed and insured Massachusetts general contractor, so your home and our crew are protected throughout the project.
