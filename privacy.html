Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Murray Home Improvement (“we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you submit our quote or contact form, we collect the details you provide — typically your name, phone number, email address, and a description of your project. We do not collect this information unless you choose to send it.

How we use it

We use your information solely to respond to your inquiry, prepare an estimate, and communicate with you about your project. We never sell or rent your information, and we only share it with service providers (such as our form-delivery provider) as needed to operate this website.

Form delivery

Form submissions are delivered to us by email through a third-party form service. Your submission is transmitted to that provider only to relay your message to us.

Cookies & analytics

This site uses minimal cookies. If analytics are enabled in the future, they will be used only to understand site usage in aggregate, never to identify individual visitors.

Your choices

You may request that we delete the information you submitted at any time by contacting us. To opt out of further communication, simply let us know.
