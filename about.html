The owner

Meet Eric Murray

When you hire Murray Home Improvement, you work directly with Eric from the first estimate to the final walk-through. There are no call centers, no rotating crews, and no guesswork — just one experienced craftsman who answers the phone and stands behind every detail.

Eric founded the company on a simple idea: treat every home like it’s your own, and every client like a neighbor. Three decades later, most of our work still comes from word of mouth and repeat clients — the truest measure of a job done right.

What we believe

Quality materials, honest estimates, and craftsmanship you can see and touch. If you can think it, we will build it — and we’ll build it to last.
