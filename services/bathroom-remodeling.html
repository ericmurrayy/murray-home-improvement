Overview

Bathroom Remodeling done right

From a tired hall bath to a spa-like primary suite, we build bathrooms that feel calm, function flawlessly, and hold up to daily use for decades.

Questions

Frequently asked

How much does a bathroom remodel cost?+

It depends on size and finishes. We provide a fair, itemized estimate with a clear breakdown of costs and materials so there are no surprises.

Can you do a curbless or walk-in shower?+

Absolutely — barrier-free and walk-in showers are popular for both style and aging-in-place, and we handle the slope and drainage correctly.

Will you handle the tile work?+

Yes. Tile is finish carpentry to us — layouts are planned and set by hand for clean, lasting results.

Do you give itemized estimates for a bathroom remodel?+

Yes. Every estimate is free and itemized, with a clear breakdown of costs and materials, so there are no surprises. Eric reviews each request personally and typically responds within one business day.

Where do you do bathroom remodeling?+

Chelmsford, including North Chelmsford, and the five towns that border it: Lowell, Westford, Tyngsborough, Billerica and Carlisle. We keep our work close to home so Eric can be on site every day.
