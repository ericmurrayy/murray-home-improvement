Overview

Home Additions done right

When you love your neighborhood but need more room, an addition is often the smartest move. We build single-room bump-outs, family rooms, in-law suites, and full multi-room additions.

Every addition is engineered to match your home’s rooflines, siding, and trim so it reads as original — not bolted-on. We manage design, permits, foundation, framing, and finish from start to finish.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do you handle permits and inspections?+

Yes. As a licensed MA general contractor we pull the permits and coordinate inspections so the addition is fully code-compliant.

Will the addition match my existing home?+

That’s the goal every time — we match rooflines, siding profiles, and trim so the new space looks original.

Can we stay in the house during construction?+

In most cases yes. We seal off the work area and keep the rest of your home livable throughout.
