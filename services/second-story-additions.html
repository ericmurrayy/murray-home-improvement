Overview

Second-Story Additions done right

When your lot is tight or you want to keep the yard, building up is the answer. We add full second stories, dormers, and partial upper levels to ranches and capes across the region.

These are major projects, and we treat them that way — careful structural engineering, weather protection, and tight scheduling so your home is reopened to the sky for as little time as possible.

What’s included

Full second levels

Add bedrooms, baths, and a primary suite above your existing footprint.

Dormers

Shed and gable dormers that add headroom and light.

Structural work

Engineered beams and reinforced framing to carry the new load.

Weather protection

Tight sequencing and tarping to keep your home dry.

Matching exterior

New roofline, siding, and windows blended to the original.

Mechanicals

Heat, electrical, and plumbing run to the new level.

Questions

Frequently asked

Can my house support a second story?+

Most can with the right structural work. We assess the foundation and framing and engineer the upgrades needed before we build.

How long are we exposed to the weather?+

We plan the open-roof phase carefully and tarp nightly — the goal is to get you weather-tight as fast as possible.

Is this cheaper than moving?+

For many families, yes — and you keep the neighborhood, schools, and yard you already love.

Do you give itemized estimates for a second-story addition?+

Yes. Every estimate is free and itemized, with a clear breakdown of costs and materials, so there are no surprises. Eric reviews each request personally and typically responds within one business day.

Where do you do second-story additions?+

Chelmsford, including North Chelmsford, and the five towns that border it: Lowell, Westford, Tyngsborough, Billerica and Carlisle. We keep our work close to home so Eric can be on site every day.
