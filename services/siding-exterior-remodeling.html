Questions

Frequently asked

What siding lasts the longest?+

Fiber-cement and quality vinyl both offer decades of low-maintenance life. We’ll match the product to your home and budget.

Will new siding improve efficiency?+

It can — we add house-wrap and can layer in insulation while the walls are exposed for a tighter envelope.

Can you match my existing trim?+

Yes, matching and upgrading trim profiles is part of doing the job right.

Do you give itemized estimates for a siding project?+

Yes. Every estimate is free and itemized, with a clear breakdown of costs and materials, so there are no surprises. Eric reviews each request personally and typically responds within one business day.

Where do you do siding & exterior remodeling?+

Chelmsford, including North Chelmsford, and the five towns that border it: Lowell, Westford, Tyngsborough, Billerica and Carlisle. We keep our work close to home so Eric can be on site every day.
